After studying in Spain, Ruomei Li returned to Taiwan, married, and made her home in the Matsu Islands. Jse-Hui Wu studied in San Francisco and, after marrying, settled in Los Angeles. Having weathered the many trials of life, the two artists decided to join forces and present this joint exhibition.
